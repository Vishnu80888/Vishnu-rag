# ProfileSity Virtual Education Expos

Virtual education fairs let students connect with universities, attend live sessions and explore programs from anywhere.

Numbers: 50+ expos per year, 500+ universities, 100% free access.
- Virtual booths: explore university stands from anywhere.
- Free to attend: register once and join every live fair.
- Students can reserve a spot at a live fair, meet admissions teams and book one-to-one appointments with universities.
- Registration is free at https://portal.codejobz.com/register and gives updates about new expos, live sessions and opportunities.
