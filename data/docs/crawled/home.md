How long does the entire admission process take? 

The typical timeline from profile assessment to receiving an admission offer is 8-12 weeks. This includes university matching (1-2 weeks), application preparation (4-6 weeks), and university processing time (2-4 weeks). Visa processing adds another 4-8 weeks depending on the country. 

What documents do I need to get started? 

To begin your journey, you need academic transcripts, standardized test scores (GRE/GMAT/IELTS/TOEFL), a current resume, and a clear idea of your target programs. Our counselors will guide you through gathering additional documents like recommendation letters and financial statements. 

Which countries and universities do you work with? 

We have partnerships with 500+ universities across USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Ireland, and Singapore. This includes top-ranked institutions like MIT, Stanford, Oxford, Cambridge, and many others. Profi matches you with universities where you have the highest acceptance probability. 

Are scholarship applications included in the pricing? 

Yes! Both our Starter and Premium plans include scholarship matching and guidance. Our Premium plan offers dedicated scholarship application support with up to 5 scholarship applications included. Many of our students have secured partial or full scholarships through our platform.
