# ProfileSity Universities

Students can discover verified university profiles from around the world, compare programs, explore campuses and apply directly through ProfileSity.

Numbers: 500+ universities, 50+ countries, 10K+ programs.
- Verified profiles: every university is vetted by the ProfileSity team.
- Direct applications: students apply without leaving ProfileSity.
- Every profile is reviewed by the team. Opening a profile shows programs, rankings and an apply option.
