# ProfileSity - Your Global Education Journey, Connected

ProfileSity (also written Profilesity) is a student-centered study-abroad platform. It connects students with universities, advisors and opportunities worldwide. Its AI study abroad advisor is called Profi.

## Headline numbers
- 500+ universities
- 50+ countries
- 98% visa success
- 25K+ students placed
- 10K+ programs indexed
- 50+ verified counselors (live advisors)

## Core platform capabilities
- Profi match: a ranked shortlist of universities in seconds.
- Scholarships: funding matched to the student.
- Courses: 10K+ programs indexed.
- Applications: students can apply without leaving the platform.
- Visa & SOP support: 98% approval rate.
- Live advisors: verified counselors.

## AI features
**AI University Matching:** the AI scores a student's profile against 500+ universities and ranks where the student has the highest real admission probability.
**AI Application Screening:** instant SOP, essay and document review with red-flag detection, so issues can be fixed before an admissions officer sees them.
**Smart Scholarship Engine:** automatically matches students to scholarships and financial aid they actually qualify for, ranked by award size and fit.

## Featured top universities
Harvard, MIT, Stanford, Oxford, Cambridge, University of Toronto, Melbourne, NUS Singapore, ETH Zurich, Imperial College.

## End-to-end study abroad services
1. University Discovery & Matching: AI-powered matching based on profile, preferences and career goals. Includes profile analysis, smart recommendations and admission probability.
2. Application Guidance: expert support for applications, SOPs and essays. Includes SOP review, essay editing and document preparation.
3. Visa Assistance: complete visa support from documentation to interview preparation. Includes a document checklist, mock interviews and embassy guidance.
4. Scholarship & Financial Aid: scholarships and aid tailored to the student. Includes scholarship matching, aid applications and budget planning.

## Process: from dream to destination
1. Profile Assessment (1-2 days): analysis of academic background, interests and career goals to create a personalized roadmap.
2. University Matching (3-5 days): AI-powered matching with universities that fit the profile, budget and aspirations.
3. Application & Visa (4-8 weeks): end-to-end support for applications, documentation and visa processing.
