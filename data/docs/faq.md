# ProfileSity Frequently Asked Questions

## How long does the entire admission process take?
The typical timeline from profile assessment to receiving an admission offer is 8-12 weeks. This includes university matching (1-2 weeks), application preparation (4-6 weeks), and university processing time (2-4 weeks). Visa processing adds another 4-8 weeks depending on the country.

## What documents do I need to get started?
To begin, you need academic transcripts, standardized test scores (GRE/GMAT/IELTS/TOEFL), a current resume, and a clear idea of your target programs. Counselors will guide you through gathering additional documents like recommendation letters and financial statements.

## Which countries and universities do you work with?
Partnerships with 500+ universities across the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Ireland and Singapore. This includes top-ranked institutions like MIT, Stanford, Oxford and Cambridge. Profi matches students with universities where they have the highest acceptance probability.

## Are scholarship applications included in the pricing?
Yes. Both the Starter and Premium plans include scholarship matching and guidance. The Premium plan offers dedicated scholarship application support with up to 5 scholarship applications included. Many students have secured partial or full scholarships through the platform.
