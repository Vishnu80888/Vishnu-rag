# About ProfileSity

Mission: democratising access to international education. ProfileSity connects ambitious students everywhere with verified universities, trusted advisors and AI-powered tools, turning the maze of studying abroad into a clear, guided path from discovery to visa.

Key numbers: 50+ countries, 500+ universities, 98% visa success.

## Our story
ProfileSity was founded on the belief that every student deserves access to quality guidance when pursuing international education, regardless of background, budget or location. The global platform brings together verified universities, trusted education advisors and AI-powered tools to help students from first shortlist to enrolment and visa.

## Our values
- Trust & verification: every university and advisor passes rigorous verification.
- Student-first: student success and wellbeing come first in every decision.
- Global access: quality guidance for students in every region across 50+ countries.
- Innovation: AI and modern tooling applied to real admissions problems.

## The AI advantage
- AI university matching: scores the profile against 500+ universities and ranks where admission probability is highest.
- AI application screening: instant SOP, essay and application checks with issue detection.
- Smart scholarship engine: matches students to scholarships and aid they qualify for, ranked by award size and fit.
- AI document analysis: transcripts, financial papers and visa documents are parsed and validated in seconds, flagging gaps before deadlines.

Call to action: students can create a free profile at the ProfileSity portal (https://portal.codejobz.com/login).
